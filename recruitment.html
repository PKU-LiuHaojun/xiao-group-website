北京大学材料科学与工程学院肖勋课题组博士后招聘启事

时间：2025-09-05

一、课题组介绍

北京大学材料科学与工程学院肖勋课题组聚焦于高性能杂化光电半导体材料与器件研究，致力于设计并制备基于有机-无机杂化材料的先进光电器件，以服务于辐射检测与能源领域的创新应用。课题组为积极探索高分辨射线探测器技术在医疗成像、工业探伤及特种化应用，开发实用性强、性能突出的高分辨射线探测器，根据科研工作需要，现拟招聘相关研究方向的博士后1-2名，欢迎国内外具有相关研究背景的青年才俊加盟。本招聘长期有效。

课题组负责人简介：肖勋，北京大学材料科学与工程学院研究员（PI，博士生导师），海外高层次人才计划获得者。长期从事杂化光电半导体材料与器件研究，共发表论文40余篇，其中以第一作者（含共同）发表 Nature（2篇）、Nat. Commun.（2篇）、Sci. Adv.（1篇）、Adv. Mater.（2篇）等，获得“国家优秀自费留学生奖学金”“UNC院长杰出博士论文奖”“欧盟玛丽居里学者”等荣誉。

课题组主要研究方向包括高分辨射线探测器、高性能光电探测器，以及用于高端光电器件的功能性封装材料。

二、博士后岗位要求

  1. 已经或即将取得国内外知名高校及科研院所的物理、化学、材料、机械自动化等理工科博士学位
  2. 具备光电探测器制备、表征或光电探测器的自动化机器人应用等方向研究经验的优先录取
  3. 品行端正，富有责任感与团队合作精神，具有独立科研能力，工作态度积极热情
  4. 较强的中英文交流写作能力
  5. 符合北京大学博士后管理相关规定

三、博士后岗位待遇

博士后基本待遇按照北京大学博士后管理办法执行，待遇条件具有竞争力：按照国家和学校有关规定，提供住房公积金、物业补贴、养老保险、职业年金、医疗、失业保险、工伤保险等福利待遇，解决博士后期间子女入托、入学问题；提供博士后公寓或6万元/年租房补贴；为符合条件者提供1.2万元/年住房补贴；鼓励并积极支持申请北京大学“博雅”博士后项目、“博士后创新人才支持计划”或北京大学材料科学与工程学院彤程博士后项目等资助。其中博雅博士后基本年薪20万，彤程博士后基本年薪16万，导师自筹博士后基本年薪12万。对于特别优秀的申请者，课题组将给予额外补助。

四、应聘材料

  1. 详细的个人简历，包括学习经历、工作经历、主要学术成果
  2. 1-2篇代表作
  3. 博士后研究计划书
  4. 推荐人（1-2名）联系方式

Postdoctoral Positions in the Xiao Group
School of Materials Science and Engineering, Peking University

Posted: September 5, 2025

1. About the Group

The Xiao Group at the School of Materials Science and Engineering, Peking University, studies high-performance hybrid optoelectronic semiconductor materials and devices. We design and fabricate advanced optoelectronic devices based on organic–inorganic hybrid materials for applications in radiation detection and energy. To develop practical, high-performance, high-resolution radiation detectors for medical imaging, industrial inspection and specialized applications, we are recruiting one or two postdoctoral researchers with relevant research experience. We welcome applications from candidates in China and abroad. Applications are accepted on a rolling basis.

Group leader: Xun Xiao is a principal investigator and doctoral supervisor at the School of Materials Science and Engineering, Peking University, and a recipient of an overseas high-level talent award. His research focuses on hybrid optoelectronic semiconductor materials and devices. He has published more than 40 papers, including first-author or co-first-author papers in Nature (2), Nature Communications (2), Science Advances (1) and Advanced Materials (2). His honors include the Chinese Government Award for Outstanding Self-financed Students Abroad, the UNC Dean’s Distinguished Dissertation Award and a Marie Curie Fellowship.

The group’s main research areas include high-resolution radiation detectors, high-performance photodetectors and functional encapsulation materials for advanced optoelectronic devices.

2. Candidate Requirements

  1. A PhD, obtained or expected shortly, in physics, chemistry, materials science, mechanical engineering, automation or a related science or engineering discipline from a recognized university or research institution.
  2. Preference will be given to candidates with experience in photodetector fabrication and characterization or photodetector applications in automation and robotics.
  3. Integrity, a strong sense of responsibility, a collaborative approach, the ability to conduct independent research and enthusiasm for research.
  4. Strong communication and writing skills in both Chinese and English.
  5. Eligibility under Peking University’s regulations for postdoctoral appointments.

3. Compensation and Benefits

Compensation and benefits follow Peking University’s postdoctoral policies. In accordance with national and university regulations, benefits include housing provident fund contributions, a property-management subsidy, pension insurance, an occupational annuity, medical insurance, unemployment insurance and work-related injury insurance. Assistance is available for children’s nursery and school enrollment during the appointment.

Postdoctoral housing or a rental subsidy of RMB 60,000 per year is provided. Eligible researchers may also receive a housing allowance of RMB 12,000 per year. Applicants are encouraged and supported to apply for Peking University’s Boya Postdoctoral Fellowship, the Postdoctoral Innovative Talent Support Program or the Tongcheng Postdoctoral Program at the School of Materials Science and Engineering.

The basic annual salary is RMB 200,000 under the Boya program, RMB 160,000 under the Tongcheng program and RMB 120,000 for supervisor-funded postdoctoral appointments. Additional support from the group is available for exceptionally qualified candidates.

4. Application Materials

  1. A detailed CV, including education, employment history and major research achievements.
  2. One or two representative publications.
  3. A proposed postdoctoral research plan.
  4. Contact details for one or two referees.
