高性能光电探测器与射线探测器High-Performance Photodetectors & Radiation Detectors

面向弱光探测、光谱识别和 X 射线成像等应用，研究钙钛矿及相关半导体材料中的缺陷调控、界面物理与载流子输运规律，发展薄膜、单晶和柔性阵列等多种器件形态。通过材料设计、晶体生长、器件结构与读出方式的协同优化，提升探测器的灵敏度、信噪比、线性动态范围、响应速度和长期稳定性，推动高性能探测技术在医学成像、环境监测和先进感知中的应用。We study defect control, interface physics and charge transport in perovskites and related semiconductors for low-light detection, spectral recognition and X-ray imaging. By coordinating materials design, crystal growth, device architecture and signal readout across thin films, single crystals and flexible arrays, we aim to improve sensitivity, signal-to-noise ratio, linear dynamic range, response speed and operational stability for medical imaging, environmental monitoring and advanced sensing.

氩等离子体表面调控与快速光电探测Argon-plasma surface engineering for fast photodetection

通过调节钙钛矿表面组成与缺陷状态，实现纳秒尺度的快速光电响应。Plasma-controlled surface composition and defect states enable nanosecond-scale photodetection.

低噪声大面积钙钛矿 X 射线探测与成像Low-noise, large-area perovskite X-ray detection and imaging

异质结结构抑制暗电流与离子迁移，在低剂量条件下获得清晰的 X 射线成像信号。Heterojunction engineering suppresses dark current and ion migration for clear imaging at low X-ray dose rates.

柔性大面积直接转换 X 射线探测器阵列Flexible, large-area direct-conversion X-ray detector arrays

以钙钛矿填充膜兼顾机械柔性、有效电荷输运和大面积加工，构建可层压的直接转换 X 射线探测阵列。Perovskite-filled membranes combine mechanical flexibility, effective charge transport and scalable processing for laminated direct-conversion X-ray detector arrays.

2.

智能光电器件与集成传感系统Smart Optoelectronic Devices & Integrated Sensing Systems

面向健康监测、环境感知和智能交互等应用，研究高性能光电探测器、柔性器件、无线电子学与信号处理的协同集成。通过可调谐光谱响应、微型化器件设计和多参数连续采集，构建从光电信号获取、无线传输到信息分析的一体化智能器件，为可穿戴与植入式监测、化学与生物传感以及新型人机交互提供器件基础。For health monitoring, environmental perception and intelligent interaction, we integrate high-performance photodetectors with flexible devices, wireless electronics and signal processing. Tunable spectral response, miniaturized device design and continuous multiparameter acquisition connect optical sensing, wireless communication and information analysis in smart devices for wearable and implantable monitoring, chemical and biological sensing, and emerging human–machine interfaces.

无线植入式光学探头与连续生理监测Wireless implantable optical probes for continuous physiological monitoring

将微型光源、光电探测器、柔性植入探头和蓝牙无线模块集成为连续组织氧合监测系统。Microscale light sources, photodetectors, a flexible implantable probe and a Bluetooth module form an integrated system for continuous tissue-oxygenation monitoring.

可调谐钙钛矿光电探测器与光学传感Tunable perovskite photodetectors for optical sensing

利用宽带与窄带可调谐光电探测器实现氧气、溶解氧和葡萄糖等分析物的快速光学检测。Broadband and narrowband tunable photodetectors enable rapid optical detection of analytes including gaseous oxygen, dissolved oxygen and glucose.

3.

功能高分子与凝胶材料Functional Polymers & Gel Materials

围绕高韧弹性体与功能离子凝胶，研究氢键、分子间作用和链缠结对材料力学性能与功能特性的协同调控，并将高分子网络设计延伸至光电器件封装防护与环境安全。We study how hydrogen bonding, intermolecular interactions and chain entanglements cooperatively regulate the mechanics and functions of tough elastomers and functional ionogels, extending polymer-network design to optoelectronic-device encapsulation, protection and environmental safety.

高韧抗裂弹性体Tough and crack-resistant elastomers

通过平衡链间氢键作用与侧链缠结，构建兼具高强度、大变形能力和抗裂性能的 PHEA 弹性体。材料表现出超过常规 PDMS 两个数量级的断裂能，并具有快速自愈合能力。Balancing interchain hydrogen bonding with side-chain entanglement produces a PHEA elastomer combining strength, large deformation and crack resistance, with fracture energy two orders of magnitude above conventional PDMS and rapid self-healing.

吸铅离子凝胶封装Lead-adsorbing ionogel encapsulation

将具有铅离子结合位点的可拉伸、自愈合离子凝胶引入钙钛矿组件封装，在保持器件效率和长期稳定性的同时，显著抑制受损组件在水环境中的铅泄漏。A stretchable, self-healing ionogel containing lead-binding sites is integrated into perovskite-module encapsulation, preserving device efficiency and long-term stability while strongly suppressing lead leakage from damaged modules in water.
